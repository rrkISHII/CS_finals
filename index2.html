PURGATORIO

Play

Purgatorio is an action RPG on Roblox, which takes inspiration from Project Moon and their games. The games focus is mainly on PvE with a strong emphasis on co-op, though working solo is possible. You will fulfill your duties as a "Fixer" either alone or with a group, though working jobs takes time. And when enough time passes your office will be forced to pay their taxes. Failing to pay said taxes will result in dire consequences.
